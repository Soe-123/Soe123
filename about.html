Sam Ocean Eccles (S.O.E) is an artist based in London.

Primarily working in oil, Sam explores different methods of distilling a landscape through structure and colour. His En Plein Air series, created on location, often uses paths and desire lines to establish structure, while the rest of the painting hovers between abstraction and the depiction of a central motif. While his Amplified Scenes series is developed in the studio, relying on impasto and chroma to create moments of focus that are heavily exaggerated from reality.
